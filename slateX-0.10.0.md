# slateX 0.10.0

## A guided tour

First launch now offers an optional walkthrough — 23 steps across four
tiers, from "type a sentence and watch the PDF" to arXiv export. It opens
a practice project of its own (a main file and two chapters, so tabs and
find-in-project are things you can actually try) and lives in the side
panel beside your document rather than in a window over it.

Every step says where the button is, not just that there is one. Several
have a **Try it** that does the thing for you. Moving on ticks the step
off; there is no checkbox to remember.

At the end it asks whether you want to start a real project or keep
playing, and tells you how to get back to both.

Replay it any time from ⌘K ▸ Guided Tour.

## Switching projects

Two ways, where before there were none short of File ▸ Open Folder:

- **⌘K ▸ Switch Project** — recents, templates, or any folder.
- **Click the slateX mark** at the top of the sidebar.

## Smaller things

- The side panel shows two tabs, never three: the tour borrows the
  library's slot while it is around and hands it straight back.
- Some quiet fixes to the tour's own progress bar and tab badge.
